Room planning

A few things to know.

Can an existing room become a vault room?

It may be possible. We review the room construction, floor conditions, door opening, and access route to understand what work the space needs.

Can you work with my builder?

Yes. We can coordinate the vault door, opening, delivery, and installation requirements with your builder so the room work follows a clear plan.

Does a vault door determine the rating of the room?

The door and the surrounding room are considered separately. Any fire or security rating needs to be supported by the documentation for the product and construction assembly.
