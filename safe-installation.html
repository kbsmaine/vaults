Common placement questions

Every home has its details.

Can my safe go downstairs or upstairs?

That depends on its weight, the full route, the stairs and landings, and the floor conditions. We review those details before confirming a placement; some locations may need structural input.

Can you anchor a safe over radiant heat?

The tubing location and available attachment points need to be established first. Let us know about radiant heat when you describe the project so anchoring can be planned appropriately.

Is curbside freight the same as installation?

Curbside delivery usually ends outside the home. Share the seller’s delivery terms so receiving, movement into the home, and final placement can be coordinated.

Have a safe and a spot in mind?

Share the product model, your ZIP code, and the intended location to begin planning.
