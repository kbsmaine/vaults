How it works

From an idea
to a finished installation.

Whether you are planning a full vault room or placing one safe, the process starts with your space. We gather the details needed for the next decision as the project develops.

01

Tell us about the project

Start with your ZIP code, project type, and the space you have in mind. A product link or rough room dimensions are helpful if you have them.

02

Review the space and equipment

We review product details, photos, and measurements, then identify whether a site visit or additional information is needed. Access and fit are considered early.

03

Agree on the work

Your proposal sets out the work, product supply where included, site preparation, pricing, and scheduling arrangements. We clarify the parts that need your builder or another trade.

04

Prepare for installation

We coordinate equipment readiness and confirm the delivery arrangements, access route, and work area. You know what needs to be ready before the installation is scheduled.

05

Install and walk through

We complete the agreed installation, review fit and operation, and leave you with the relevant care and product information. Any remaining items are made clear.
