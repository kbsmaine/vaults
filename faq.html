Getting started

Your project, from the beginning.

What does Northwoods Vault & Safe do?

We install indoor vault rooms and gun safes, and supply and install vault doors. We help plan the installation around your home, your equipment, and the way you intend to use the space.

Where do you work?

We serve homeowners throughout Maine and New Hampshire. Share your town and a few project details so we can discuss the next step.

Do you sell vault doors?

Yes. We supply and install vault doors sourced through Safe & Vault Store. We can help review size, door swing, finish, and installation requirements before you choose.

Can you install a gun safe I already own?

Contact us with the make, model, dimensions, weight, and planned location. We will review the access route and installation needs with you. For a new safe, it helps to have this conversation before you order.

When should I get in touch?

Early is helpful, especially before framing a vault door opening or ordering equipment. If your home is already finished, we can start by discussing the space and access you have.

Planning & installation

Making it work for your home.

Can you work with my builder?

Yes. We can coordinate installation details with your builder during a new build or renovation, including the door opening, room preparation, and installation timing.

Can a safe go in a basement?

It depends on the safe and the route. Weight, stair construction, landing space, turns, and headroom all matter. We review those details before confirming whether the location will work.

What if I have radiant floor heat?

Let us know during planning. Any anchoring needs to account for the floor construction and the location of heating tubes or other services.

How do I compare fire and security ratings?

Start with the manufacturer’s documentation for the exact model you are considering. Door and safe specifications vary, and a vault room’s construction also matters. We can help identify the product information relevant to your installation.

Costs & next steps

The practical details.

How much will my project cost?

The equipment, access route, preparation, and installation needs shape the price. Send your town, project type, and any equipment details you have so we can begin discussing your project.

Can we start with photos and measurements?

Yes. They can help us understand the space and identify the next questions. We will let you know if a site visit or additional measurements are needed before confirming the work.

How far ahead should I plan?

Timing depends on the project, product availability, and our installation schedule. Share your preferred timeframe when you contact us, along with any construction or delivery dates.

What should I include in my first message?

Your town, the service you are interested in, your preferred timeframe, and any known product details are a good start. Please keep combinations, access codes, and detailed inventories private.

Have a question about your home?

Tell us what you are planning. We will help you understand the next step.
