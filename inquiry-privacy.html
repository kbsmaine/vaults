Your inquiry information.

Preparing your inquiry

The planner asks for your name, email, optional phone, project ZIP, service, optional floor, and general project description. Preparing or editing the summary does not send this information. No customer account is required.

Sending your inquiry

When online sending is available and you choose Send inquiry, your contact fields and edited summary are transmitted over HTTPS to Formspree. Editing the summary does not remove the original contact fields from the submission. Formspree processes the request, checks it for spam, retains a submission record under the selected service settings, and sends a notification to Northwoods’ private business inbox.

Northwoods uses your inquiry to review your project and respond. Formspree also receives technical information such as your IP address, browser information, and website origin to operate and protect its service. See Formspree’s privacy policy. The website does not display the receiving mailbox.

Drafts and duplicate prevention

Your draft stays in page memory; the website does not save its contents in browser storage. Closing or refreshing the page can discard the draft. A small submission-status marker, containing only a status and timestamp, is stored in this browser tab when a send starts. It helps prevent repeat submissions after a reload, expires after 24 hours, and is normally removed when the tab closes. If browser storage is unavailable, this protection applies only while the current page remains open.

If sending fails

The form keeps your fields and edited summary on the page. A confirmed rejection can be retried. If a connection problem means we cannot tell whether the request arrived, another send is disabled in this tab to reduce duplicates. Keep a copy and ask Northwoods to check before submitting again. A submission confirmation means the form service accepted the request, not that an installation is booked or an email has been read.

Keep details general

Do not include safe combinations, alarm codes, a detailed firearm inventory, payment details, or your street address. A ZIP code and general description are sufficient for this first conversation.

Access and questions

Authorized Northwoods staff use the inbox and form-service dashboard to review inquiries. Email and form-service copies follow their account settings; closing the browser does not delete a sent inquiry. Ask Northwoods in the resulting project conversation about correcting or deleting information. This inquiry does not automatically create a customer portal account.
