More answers ↗
Do I need to choose a vault door first?

You can start with your goals and the space available. We supply and install vault doors, so door selection and installation planning can be considered together.

Can a safe go in a basement or upstairs?

That depends on the safe’s weight, the route through your home, and the floor conditions. Share the intended location so those details can be reviewed before a placement is planned.

What do you need to get started?

Your ZIP code, the type of project, and a short description are a useful start. If you already have a safe or door in mind, include the model and approximate dimensions.

Do you serve both states statewide?

Yes. Our service area includes all of Maine and New Hampshire. Travel, seasonal access, and site conditions are considered when planning each project.
